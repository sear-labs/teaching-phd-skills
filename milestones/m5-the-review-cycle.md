# M5 — The Review Cycle

**Tier:** Year 3+

Everything between "the draft is finished" and "it is out in the world." Review someone else's
work, answer a review of your own, choose where to send it, and stand up and defend it.

The first two are reciprocal and that is deliberate. **You will review a labmate's Report Draft 1
and they will review yours**, and each of you then writes the response letter to the review you
received. Nothing here is simulated.

## The four skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| Reviewing papers | reading | structured referee report, PDF |
| Response to reviewers | writing | PDF response letter |
| Venue selection and submission mechanics | writing | one-page memo + cover letter |
| Conference talk and poster | writing | slides PDF + three questions |

### This milestone is built on the course's existing structure

Report Draft 1 (feedback) and Report Draft 2 (revised) already exist as assignments. Skills 17
and 18 slot into the gap between them. Arrange your review pairing before Draft 1 is due — after
it, the timing no longer works.

## How you submit this

This submission is an **index, not new work.** All four skills were already submitted as ordinary
weekly log entries. Here you are telling me where to find them.

For each of the four skills, one line:

```
<skill name> — submitted in <WR/WW/WA number>, week of <date> — <link or "attached to that entry">
```

Then one paragraph, no more than 150 words: **which of the four was hardest, and why.**

Graded **complete / redo**. Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass; I will name
which, and you resubmit only that skill. A milestone you have completed is completed
permanently — it does not repeat in a later semester.

## Gate

- [ ] All four skills submitted, in **four different weeks**
- [ ] Every competency check on all four skills passes
- [ ] The index lists a locatable submission for each
- [ ] The reflection paragraph is present
