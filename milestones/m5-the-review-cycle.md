# M5 — The Review Cycle

**Tier:** Year 3+

Everything between "the draft is finished" and "it is out in the world." Review someone else's
work, answer a review of your own, choose where to send it, present it, and defend it under
questioning.

These are one skill at five temperatures: **your work meeting other people.** The first two are
reciprocal by design — **you referee a labmate's Report Draft 1 and they referee yours**, and
each of you then writes the response letter to the review you received. Nothing here is
simulated.

## The five skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| Reviewing papers | reading | structured referee report, PDF |
| Response to reviewers | writing | PDF response letter |
| Venue selection and submission mechanics | writing | one-page memo plus cover letter |
| Conference talk and poster | writing | slides PDF plus three questions |
| Defending your work | writing | three steelmanned objections, delivered live |

### This milestone is built on the course's existing structure

Report Draft 1 (feedback) and Report Draft 2 (revised) already exist as assignments. Reviewing
and responding slot into the gap between them. **Arrange your review pairing before Draft 1 is
due** — after it, the timing no longer works.

### Defending your work does not wait for Year 3

Nothing in this curriculum is gated, and this is the skill where that matters most. Its only real
prerequisite is *claim, reason, evidence* in M3. **If you are proposing next semester, take it
now** — it is the one skill with a hard external deadline attached, and rehearsing after the
defence is worth nothing.

## How you submit this

This submission is an **index, not new work.** Every skill was already submitted as an ordinary
weekly log entry. Here you are telling me where to find them.

For each skill, one line:

```
<skill name> — submitted in <WR/WW/WA number>, week of <date> — <link or "attached to that entry">
```

Then one paragraph, no more than 150 words: **which was hardest, and why.**

Graded **complete / redo**. Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass; I will name
which, and you resubmit only that skill. A milestone you have completed is completed
permanently — it does not repeat in a later semester.

## Gate

- [ ] Every skill submitted, each in a **different week**
- [ ] Every competency check on every skill passes
- [ ] The index lists a locatable submission for each
- [ ] The reflection paragraph is present
