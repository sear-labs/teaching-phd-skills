# M6 — Stewardship

**Tier:** Year 3+

The loosest grouping of the six, and worth saying so plainly. What joins these four is that each
produces something that **outlasts you in this lab**: a citable deposit, a dataset somebody can
still find terms for in four years, an idea written well enough to be funded, and a person who
learned to do research because you scoped their first task properly.

This is the year-3+ milestone because all four assume you have something finished worth
depositing, defending, or handing on.

## The four skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| Zenodo deposit | arithmetic | Zenodo record URL |
| Research data management | arithmetic | one-page data statement |
| Proposal writing fragments | writing | PDF to the call's page limits |
| Mentoring an undergraduate | writing | task brief + retrospective |

### Ordering

**Zenodo before research data management** is the wrong way round intuitively, but it is correct:
doing the deposit first makes the data statement concrete, because you have just discovered which
files you were not certain you could publish. Doing the deposit *without* having thought about it
is the failure the second skill prevents — so deposit something you are certain about first.

### Mentoring takes a semester

Do not leave it to week 12. It requires an actual REU or UROP student and an actual handoff. If
you do not currently supervise anyone, talk to me early in the semester rather than late.

## How you turn this in

**Nothing new gets made for this.** All four skills were already submitted as ordinary
weekly log entries, each one replacing that week's WR, WW or WA. This submission just tells me
where to find them.

**Paste this straight into the text box of this assignment.** No attachment is needed — the
artifacts are already sitting in the weekly entries you link to.

One line per skill: the skill, which entry it went in, the week, and where the artifact is.

```
Zenodo deposit              WA3   week of 8 Sep   github.com/you/thesis
Research data management    WA5   week of 22 Sep  same repo, see README
Proposal writing fragments  WW7   week of 6 Oct   attached to that entry
Mentoring an undergraduate  WW9   week of 20 Oct  Overleaf link in that entry
```

Then **one paragraph, 150 words maximum**: which of the four was hardest, and why. Not
what you did — you already wrote that. What was hard about it.

That is the whole submission. It should take ten minutes.

### How it is graded

**Complete / redo.** Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass. I will name which
skill and which check, and **you redo only that skill** — not the milestone, and not the others.
A milestone marked complete is complete permanently; it does not repeat in a later semester.

[**The whole curriculum on one page**](https://sear-labs.github.io/teaching-phd-skills/handbook/curriculum.html) — every skill with the exact artifact it wanted
and the checklist it was graded against. Use it to confirm you have everything before submitting.
