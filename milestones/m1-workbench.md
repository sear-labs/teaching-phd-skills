# M1 — The Workbench

**Tier:** Year 1

Four skills about getting computation done in the tools you already have open, and about the one
habit that makes AI assistance safe: **checking the output against something you knew
independently.** Every skill here ends with a verification step, and the verification is what is
being graded — not the spreadsheet, not the prompt, not the notebook.

This is the milestone most likely to feel beneath a third-year student. Take it anyway if you
have not: the checking habit is the thing, and it does not arrive on its own.

## The four skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Excel | arithmetic | .xlsx in a WA entry |
| AI prompting | arithmetic | text entry in a WA entry |
| Excel with AI assist | arithmetic | .xlsx plus explanation, WA entry |
| Colab with AI assist | arithmetic | Colab share link, WA entry |

### Order

Take **AI prompting** before the two "with AI assist" skills. The other ordering works but wastes
the exercise.

## How you submit this

This submission is an **index, not new work.** All four skills were already submitted as ordinary
weekly log entries. Here you are telling me where to find them.

For each of the four skills, one line:

```
<skill name> — submitted in <WR/WW/WA number>, week of <date> — <link or "attached to that entry">
```

Then one paragraph, no more than 150 words: **which of the four was hardest, and why.**

Graded **complete / redo**. Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass; I will name
which, and you resubmit only that skill. A milestone you have completed is completed
permanently — it does not repeat in a later semester.

## Gate

- [ ] All four skills submitted, in **four different weeks**
- [ ] Every competency check on all four skills passes
- [ ] The index lists a locatable submission for each
- [ ] The reflection paragraph is present
