# M3 — Reading and Citing

**Tier:** Year 2

Four skills about taking the literature seriously: reading it for what it actually shows, finding
the place where it stops, citing it for claims it genuinely makes, and crediting the people who
helped you honestly.

The last two belong together for a reason that is not obvious at first. **Whether a citation is
real and whether the credit is fair are the same question asked about different people.** Both
are cheap to get right in advance and expensive to fix once a paper is out.

## The four skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| Reading and critiquing papers | reading | WR entry, 300-500 words |
| Literature review and gap-finding | reading | PDF: map + gap paragraph |
| Reference manager and citation verification | reading | table + updated draft |
| Research ethics and authorship | writing | CRediT statement + evidence circulated |

### Note on skill 11

The citation audit is performed on **your own draft**, and you are asked to pick the ten
references you are *least* sure of. Picking ten you remember reading defeats it. People
consistently find failures; the count is not the grade.

## How you submit this

This submission is an **index, not new work.** All four skills were already submitted as ordinary
weekly log entries. Here you are telling me where to find them.

For each of the four skills, one line:

```
<skill name> — submitted in <WR/WW/WA number>, week of <date> — <link or "attached to that entry">
```

Then one paragraph, no more than 150 words: **which of the four was hardest, and why.**

Graded **complete / redo**. Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass; I will name
which, and you resubmit only that skill. A milestone you have completed is completed
permanently — it does not repeat in a later semester.
