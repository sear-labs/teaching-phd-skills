# M4 — The Manuscript

**Tier:** Year 2

Everything that goes into assembling a document somebody else can read, trust and cite. The
argument itself was M3; this is the production of the artifact that carries it.

All five are performed on the same draft, which is the point — the structure, the references,
the credit, the figures and the alt text are five passes over one document, not five documents.

Aligns directly with **Report Draft 1 and Report Draft 2** in this course. Take it in the same
semester as the drafts and the work is shared rather than doubled.

## The five skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| IMRAD structure | writing | Overleaf link |
| Reference manager and citation verification | reading | table plus updated draft |
| Research ethics and authorship | writing | CRediT statement plus evidence circulated |
| Figure and table design | writing | PDF: before, after, standalone caption |
| Accessibility of notebooks and figures | writing | repo URL |

### Order

**IMRAD first.** Its contribution claim is the sentence the cover letter and the conference talk
both reuse later, and the other four assume a draft that has a shape.

**Take figure design and accessibility together.** They make requirements of the *same figure*
from different directions — greyscale survivability and colour-independence are one constraint
seen twice. Rebuild once, satisfying both, rather than rebuilding twice.

**Citation verification is done on your own draft**, and you are asked for the ten references you
are *least* sure of. Picking ten you remember reading defeats it. People consistently find
failures; the count is not the grade.

### Accessibility has a deadline

WCAG 2.1 AA applies to this institution's web content from **26 April 2027**. It is the one item
in this curriculum with an external date attached.

## How you turn this in

**Nothing new gets made for this.** All five skills were already submitted as ordinary
weekly log entries, each one replacing that week's WR, WW or WA. This submission just tells me
where to find them.

**Paste this straight into the text box of this assignment.** No attachment is needed — the
artifacts are already sitting in the weekly entries you link to.

One line per skill: the skill, which entry it went in, the week, and where the artifact is.

```
IMRAD structure                              WW3   week of 8 Sep   github.com/you/thesis
Reference manager and citation verification  WR5   week of 22 Sep  same repo, see README
Research ethics and authorship               WW7   week of 6 Oct   attached to that entry
Figure and table design                      WW9   week of 20 Oct  Overleaf link in that entry
Accessibility of notebooks and figures       WW11  week of 3 Nov   PDF attached to that entry
```

Then **one paragraph, 150 words maximum**: which of the five was hardest, and why. Not
what you did — you already wrote that. What was hard about it.

That is the whole submission. It should take ten minutes.

### How it is graded

**Complete / redo.** Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass. I will name which
skill and which check, and **you redo only that skill** — not the milestone, and not the others.
A milestone marked complete is complete permanently; it does not repeat in a later semester.
