# M4 — The Manuscript

**Tier:** Year 2

Everything between having results and having a document somebody else can read. These four
compound: the contribution claim from IMRAD is the sentence the cover letter and the conference
talk both reuse, and the figure you rebuild here is the figure that goes in the talk.

Aligns directly with **Report Draft 1 and Report Draft 2** in this course. Take it in the same
semester as the drafts and the work is shared rather than doubled.

## The four skills

| Skill | Channel | Turn in as |
|---|---|---|
| IMRAD structure | writing | Overleaf link |
| LaTeX and Overleaf | writing | Overleaf link + compiled PDF |
| Figure and table design | writing | PDF: before, after, standalone caption |
| Accessibility of notebooks and figures | writing | repo URL |

### Take skills 15 and 16 together

Figure design and accessibility make requirements of the **same figure**, arriving from different
directions — greyscale survivability and colour-independence are the same constraint. Rebuild
once, satisfying both, rather than rebuilding twice.

### Accessibility has a deadline

WCAG 2.1 AA applies to this institution's web content from **26 April 2027**. It is the one item
in this curriculum with an external date attached.

## How you submit this

This submission is an **index, not new work.** All four skills were already submitted as ordinary
weekly log entries. Here you are telling me where to find them.

For each of the four skills, one line:

```
<skill name> — submitted in <WR/WW/WA number>, week of <date> — <link or "attached to that entry">
```

Then one paragraph, no more than 150 words: **which of the four was hardest, and why.**

Graded **complete / redo**. Redo means one or more competency checks did not pass; I will name
which, and you resubmit only that skill. A milestone you have completed is completed
permanently — it does not repeat in a later semester.
