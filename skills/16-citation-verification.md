## Why this exists

Fabricated references are a live failure mode, not a hypothetical one — a language model will
produce a plausible author list, a plausible title, a plausible year, and a DOI that resolves to
something else entirely. The subtler and more common failure is worse: a **real** paper cited for
a claim it does not make. That one survives every automated check, and a reviewer who knows the
cited paper will find it in ninety seconds.

## External material

[Zotero documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/) for the manager itself — collection
structure, the browser connector, BibTeX export. It covers storing references. It does not cover
verifying them, and no reference manager does.

## Governing part

None.

## The mechanic

Take ten references from your own draft — the ten you are least certain about, not the ten you
remember reading.

For each, one row:

| Reference | DOI resolves? | Claim located at | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

"Claim located at" means a **page, section, or figure number** where the cited paper actually
says the thing you are citing it for. Not "it's in there somewhere." If you cannot locate it,
the reference is either wrong or the sentence in your draft is, and both are fixed the same way:
strike it and rewrite.

Then fix the draft. The table alone is an observation; the corrected draft is the deliverable.

## Deliverable

The table, plus a link to the updated draft showing the corrections.

## Competency check

- [ ] Ten rows
- [ ] Every row has a resolving DOI or a stable URL
- [ ] Every row locates the claim by page, section, or figure
- [ ] Any reference that failed is struck from the draft, and the draft link shows it
- [ ] The count of failures is stated plainly

## Turning it in

**This replaces your reading entry for the week you do it.** Submit it to that week's
**WR** assignment in Canvas — the same one you would have submitted anyway. It is not an
extra piece of work on top of the log.

- **In the text box:** how many of the ten failed, and which kind of failure each was.
- **You hand in:** A **ten-row table** — reference / does the DOI resolve / where the claim sits by page or figure / verdict — plus a link to the draft with the failures struck

You do not submit anything to the milestone yet. When all five skills in **M4** are done, submit the **M4 — The Manuscript** assignment, which is just
an index saying which week each one went in.
