## Why this exists

"Can this be deposited?" has to be answered **before** the deposit, because a deposit is
public and permanent and the undo button does not fully exist. This lab's data makes the
question live rather than theoretical: ERCOT data arrives under use agreements, industry
partners supply material flows under NDA, and DOE-funded work can carry its own handling
requirements. A student who deposits an NDA dataset has created a problem that lands on their
advisor and their sponsor, not on them.

## External material

[DMPTool](https://dmptool.org/) — templates matched to specific funders, including DOE and NSF.
Your award's own Data Management Plan, which already exists and which most students have never
read. Ask for it.

## Governing part

None directly. Skill 21's deposit is governed by the standard; what you are permitted to put in
it is governed by the agreements, which are not a code question.

## The mechanic

Write a one-page data statement for your own datasets. For each:

- **Source.** Where it came from, and under what instrument — a use agreement, a public
  download, an NDA, generated in the lab.
- **Licence.** Named. "Public data" is not a licence; CC-BY-4.0 is.
- **Restrictions**, quoted from the actual agreement rather than remembered. If you cannot find
  the agreement, that is the finding, and locating it is the assignment.
- **What is deposited and what is withheld**, and why.
- **Where the withheld version lives**, so that it is findable after you graduate.

The last line is the one that matters to the lab and the one students skip.

## Deliverable

One-page data statement, PDF.

## Competency check

- [ ] Every dataset used in your work appears
- [ ] Source named with the instrument it arrived under
- [ ] Licence named explicitly per dataset
- [ ] Any restriction quoted from the agreement, with the agreement located
- [ ] Withheld items named, with a reason and a location
